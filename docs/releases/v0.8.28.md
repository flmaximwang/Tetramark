# 0.8.28 — trim the schema skill body to its 150-line budget

The `tetramark-schema` skill body now fits the same 150-line budget as the primary skill: 149
measured body lines, down from 171, with nothing dropped. The trailing version-bump, spec 5 → 6 and
fingerprint paragraphs were already covered in `references/presets.md` and
`references/evolution.md`; the full additive/destructive classification and the tightening sequence
now live only in the references, and the body keeps a compact rule plus pointers. The schema body
budget in `tests/conftest.py` drops from 171 to 150, so the contract cannot drift back up. Docs and
test budget only: no CLI, schema, preset, migration or ledger-format change.

## What moved, and what stayed

- Kept word for word in the body: the frontmatter and the visible version line, the intro, "Where
  schemas live", the YAML skeleton with its fenced `version: 1`, the field-type and box-geometry
  tables, the rules table, all six workflow steps and the guardrails.
- The body now carries one "Additive vs destructive" rule: additive changes apply without flags,
  destructive ones need `--allow-destructive`, `renamed_from: old_name` makes a rename additive and
  data-preserving, and the body points at `references/evolution.md` and `references/presets.md`
  (`tm presets show molbio`, `tm schema show plasmid`).
- `references/evolution.md` gains an "Additive vs destructive" section with the complete additive
  list (new kind; new nullable/defaulted field; new enum value; new view/rule/test; new
  aliquot-fields table; relaxing NOT NULL) and destructive list (drop a kind/field; type change; new
  NOT NULL without a default; new UNIQUE; removing an enum value), the automatic rebuild of
  generated columns and dependent FKs, and pointers to the rename and tightening sections. The
  duplicate paragraph in "Renaming instead of drop+add" now points at that section, and step 2 of
  the tightening procedure names the backfill commands: `tm export`/`tm import`, the app, or
  `entity edit`.
- `references/presets.md` § "Version bumps and the manual spec 5 → 6 upgrade" now states the part
  the body used to carry: `migration/spec/5-to-6/README.md` remaps the old status vocabulary, and
  the operator runs `tm schema plan`/`apply` to write `spec_version`, `preset_versions`, the
  `aliquot.status` `CHECK` and the regenerated `dolt_tests`.
- The schema body budget is `("body", 150)` in `tests/conftest.py`; the rule "do not raise a budget
  — move detail into references/" is unchanged, and the primary skill stays `("file", 150)` at 150
  lines. The assert wording in `tests/test_skill_version.py` no longer names the pending trim; no
  test was weakened, skipped or re-marked, and no keyword-coverage test was added.

## Upgrade

Nothing to migrate: this release changes documentation and one test budget. No command, schema,
preset, migration step or ledger format changed; both skills are only re-stamped to 0.8.28.
