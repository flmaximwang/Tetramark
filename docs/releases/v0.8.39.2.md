# 0.8.39.2 — the aliquot add-form asks for a quantity and a unit

`tm aliquot add-form` now asks for an optional quantity and its unit, so a batch of tubes can be
created with the amount each one holds without leaving the form for `tm aliquot add --quantity Q
--unit U`. The quantity is the amount of **each** new tube — never divided across the tubes — and
the unit is asked only once a quantity is given, where it is required. Everything else is
unchanged: no schema or DDL change, no new flag, no change to `tm stock add-form`, no editing
feature, and a skipped quantity stores NULL quantity/unit exactly as before. The form contract is
in [`docs/spec/06-cli.md`](https://github.com/flmaximwang/Tetramark/blob/v0.8.39.2/docs/spec/06-cli.md)
§6.3, next to the stock form.

## What the aliquot form asks

- **The steps, in order:** kind → stock → count → quantity → unit → box → slots. Kind, stock,
  count, box and slots behave exactly as they did; count still defaults to 1 and takes a minimum
  of 1, and box/slot visibility and the slot rules are untouched.
- **quantity** is the amount in each new tube: `count 3` with `quantity 0.5` and `unit ml` writes
  three tubes of `0.5 ml` each, exactly as
  `tm aliquot add STOCK --count 3 --quantity 0.5 --unit ml` does. It is optional, has no default
  and no prefill, and **zero is allowed** — zero is a real quantity.
- **The quantity is checked as typed** (trimmed, then parsed as a decimal): a non-number is
  refused with `quantity must be a decimal number (got '…')`, and NaN, Infinity or a negative
  value with `quantity must be a finite, non-negative number (got '…')`. `0` and ` 2.5 ` are
  accepted, and the form accepts and refuses exactly what core `--quantity` accepts and refuses.
- **unit** is asked only when a quantity is given, and is then required: an empty Enter answers
  `unit is required` and keeps the step. It is free text, trimmed, 1–32 characters
  (`unit must be at most 32 characters`): no list, no spelling or case normalisation, no
  conversion, no default, no Tab completion.
- **Skipping the quantity skips the unit.** An empty Enter on the quantity step — or going back
  with Shift+Tab or a review jump and clearing it — drops any unit answer, so a unit without a
  quantity is unreachable from the form (core's `--quantity and --unit must be given together`
  stays as the backstop).
- **The write is the same one `tm aliquot add` makes:** the payload carries `quantity` and `unit`
  through to `ledger.aliquots.add(...)`, so the tubes, the per-tube amount and the create events
  are identical to `tm aliquot add --quantity Q --unit U`, and skipping the quantity writes NULL
  quantity/unit exactly as before.
- **The review screen** shows the entered quantity and unit (the unit row is absent when the
  quantity was skipped), jump-by-number works for both, and a core rejection that names quantity
  or unit returns focus to that step.

## Upgrade

Nothing to migrate. The form asks for two existing fields and calls the existing write: no
schema, DDL, preset, migration, query or dependency change, no new flag, no editing feature, and
`tm stock add-form` is untouched. The shipped skills are re-stamped to 0.8.39.2.
